Dusk Common

pad note from the MIDI common tone, still sounding at a chord change

Reading the MIDI.

Reference part

Inner motion

This list is read from the inner-motion MIDI file. It is not drawn on the pad field and it is not part of the core count. Pair motion A with pad A, or motion B with pad B.

    Pad fields use MIDI 48 to 81. The polyphony cap is 6. These studies sound two to four notes at once. Inner motion is monophonic, MIDI 64 to 84, and is a reference part, not part of the core count. C4 is 60.

    The pad polyphony cap is 6, inside the 4 to 6 range used for these pads. These studies sound two to four notes at once. Inner motion is monophonic. Same-pitch overlap inside a part is illegal.

    Analog-like describes the intended playback, a slow attack and a soft tone. It is not a MIDI property.

    Release tails are not in the MIDI. Each note ends at note-off. A synth may ring after that. The ring is not in the file.

    Eight bars for every study. Sixteen and thirty-two bar studies were allowed and were not used, because 16 bars at 60 BPM last 64 seconds and 32 bars at 60 BPM last 128 seconds.

    Studies

    s01 Dusk Common

    72 BPM, 8 bars, D major, tonic D. About 26.7 seconds at the encoded tempo.

    Four bars of D, two of G, two of D.

    A3 is the held tone in variation A. Variation B holds D4 into G, then B3 and D3 into the return.

    Play pad A with inner motion A, or pad B with inner motion B. They are alternatives. Release tails are not in the MIDI. Each note ends at note-off. A synth may ring after that. The ring is not in the file.

    s02 Open Fourths

    64 BPM, 8 bars, A natural minor, tonic A. About 30.0 seconds at the encoded tempo.

    Three bars of an A fourth, three of F, two of A.

    Variation A holds A3 into F. Variation B holds D3 and A3 into F, then C4 into the return.

    Play pad A with inner motion A, or pad B with inner motion B. They are alternatives. Release tails are not in the MIDI. Each note ends at note-off. A synth may ring after that. The ring is not in the file.

    s03 Suspended Hold

    80 BPM, 8 bars, F Lydian, tonic F. About 24.0 seconds at the encoded tempo.

    Four bars of Fsus2, two of C, two of F.

    Variation A holds F3 and C4 into C. Variation B holds A3 and C4 into C, then E4, G3, and D4 into the return.

    Play pad A with inner motion A, or pad B with inner motion B. They are alternatives. Release tails are not in the MIDI. Each note ends at note-off. A synth may ring after that. The ring is not in the file.

    s04 Low Bloom

    60 BPM, 8 bars, C Dorian, tonic C. About 32.0 seconds at the encoded tempo.

    Four bars of Cm, two of Bb, two of Cm.

    Variation A holds C3 into Bb. Variation B holds G3 and Bb3 into Bb, then F3 into the return.

    Play pad A with inner motion A, or pad B with inner motion B. They are alternatives. The E3 is one half note. It does not retune the study. Release tails are not in the MIDI. Each note ends at note-off. A synth may ring after that. The ring is not in the file.

    s05 High Window

    96 BPM, 8 bars, E Mixolydian, tonic E. About 20.0 seconds at the encoded tempo.

    Two bars of E, four of A, two of E.

    Variation A holds E3 into A. Variation B holds E3, G#4, and B4 into A, then D5 into the return.

    Play pad A with inner motion A, or pad B with inner motion B. They are alternatives. Do not fill the gap with a chord tone from another study. Release tails are not in the MIDI. Each note ends at note-off. A synth may ring after that. The ring is not in the file.

    s06 Pedal Drift

    68 BPM, 8 bars, G natural minor, tonic G. About 28.2 seconds at the encoded tempo.

    Two bars each of Gm, Cm, Eb, and Gm.

    Variation A holds G3 across every change. Variation B holds D3 across the first two changes, then G4 and Eb4.

    Play pad A with inner motion A, or pad B with inner motion B. They are alternatives. Release tails are not in the MIDI. Each note ends at note-off. A synth may ring after that. The ring is not in the file.

    s07 Two-Note Space

    84 BPM, 8 bars, Bb major, tonic Bb. About 22.9 seconds at the encoded tempo.

    Three bars of Bb, three of Eb, two of F.

    Variation A holds Bb3 into Eb. Variation B holds F3 and Bb3 into Eb.

    Play pad A with inner motion A, or pad B with inner motion B. They are alternatives. The Gb4 is one half note. Release tails are not in the MIDI. Each note ends at note-off. A synth may ring after that. The ring is not in the file.

    s08 Cadence Breath

    104 BPM, 8 bars, A major, tonic A. About 18.5 seconds at the encoded tempo.

    Three bars of D, four of A, then one silent bar.

    Variation A holds A3 into A. Variation B holds E4 into A. Nothing crosses into the silent bar.

    Play pad A with inner motion A, or pad B with inner motion B. They are alternatives. Do not fill bar 8. Release tails are not in the MIDI. Each note ends at note-off. A synth may ring after that. The ring is not in the file.
